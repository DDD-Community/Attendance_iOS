DDD Attendance App is the official attendance and schedule management app
designed for the DDD IT Community.

• QR Code Attendance Check
Managers can scan each member’s QR code to record attendance instantly.

• Centralized Schedule Management
View and manage all DDD events and sessions in one place.

• Individual Attendance Profiles
Members can track and share their attendance history through personal profiles.

• Efficient Admin Management
Organizers gain clear visibility into participation data,
ensuring transparent and efficient community operations.
